# Sweet As Box: lolly research for Christmas 2026

**Date:** 26 October 2026
**For:** Mini box ($35, contents budget $8 or less, lollies about $4) and Classic box ($59, contents budget $20 or less, lollies about $7–9)
**Earlier reports:** none. This is the first report in `sweet-as-box/research/`.

## How to read the prices

- Every price comes from a NZ retailer or wholesaler page found by web search. The supermarket, NXP and OfficeMax sites
  could not be opened directly from this environment (the lookups failed), so the prices are **search-index snapshots and
  their dates are unconfirmed**. Supermarket prices also change from store to store. **Check the live price before you buy.**
- Where several prices turned up, the table shows the range and names the cheapest source.
- NXP prices are shown **excluding GST** where the listing says so. Inc-GST figures (×1.15) are my own calculation.
- **No retailer listed bag dimensions** for any product, so "lies flat in the box" is **unconfirmed for every bag**. Buy
  one of each and test it in a Mini box (200×150×75mm) and a Classic box (225×167×83mm) before ordering in bulk.
- **Best-before dates were not shown** on any listing. The one exception is the Rainbow 20×110g carton, which lists a
  12-month shelf life. For December dispatch, check the pack shows a best-before of **late February 2027 or later**,
  which gives the 8 weeks needed.

## 2. Recommended lolly mixes

The wrapped-piece costs below use the cheapest confirmed route: the Mayceys Mints NXP bag and the retail Minties bag.

### Mini box: target about $4 for lollies (box contents $8 or less)

**Recommended: NZ-made and cheapest**

| Item | Quantity | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Rainbow Airplanes 110g, sealed bag (carton of 20) | 1 | $1.50 |
| Pascall Minties, wrapped | 3 | about $0.27 |
| Mayceys Mints, wrapped | 3 | about $0.21 |
| **Lolly total** | | **about $1.98** |

That is about **$2 under** the $4 lolly allowance. The 110g bag is the size most likely to lie flat in the Mini box.

**Upgrade: the iconic brand.** Use a Pascall Jet Planes 180g bag ($2.95) instead of the Rainbow Airplanes. The lolly
total becomes **about $3.43**, still under $4. Only do this if a test bag lies flat in a 200×150×75mm box.

Weight: about 110–180g of bag plus about 35g of wrapped pieces, well within 1kg.

## 4. Avoid list

| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Jaffas** | Discontinued in NZ by RJ's from 1 July 2025. Rainbow Confectionery said it would consider making an alternative, but I found no launch. |
| **Whittaker's Toffee Milk** | Discontinued. The Spinoff (April 2026) describes the "last box in New Zealand". |
| **Pascall Jet Planes 2kg (NXP or OfficeMax)** | Loose and not individually wrapped. Splitting it into bags is food handling and needs registration. |
| **Loose bulk-bin lollies** (Woolworths bulk Rainbow Milk Bottles, Mayceys Fini Spin Tops) | Not factory-sealed. |
| **Pik n Mix loose lollies, and LollyShop "Pick 'n' Mix" 100g bags** | Bagged by the retailer from loose stock, not the manufacturer. They're also expensive: Jet Planes 100g costs $2.99, against $2.95 for a sealed 180g bag. LollyShop's own-brand Frosted Caramels were also on an MPI recall list (gluten/wheat, best-before 04/10/2025). |
| **Plain chocolate** (Cadbury or Whittaker's blocks, Clinkers) | Melts in a summer courier van. Not even suitable for November. |
| **Large bags** (Pascall Marshmallows 520g, Pams Giant Mallows 600g, Fruit Bursts 400g as a box bag) | Too big for the box and over budget per box. Only buy the 400g Fruit Bursts to split into wrapped pieces. |
| **Pascall Snifters** | Discontinued as a lolly. Snifter Lumps (chocolate) appear to be out of stock. |

## Cheaper routes and suppliers

- **Rainbow Confectionery, Ōamaru, direct.** Cartons of 20×110g bags cost $30 ($1.50 a bag) for Airplanes and Party Mix,
  with a 12-month shelf life. For more than 12 cartons, contact them. Factory shop: 459 Thames Highway, Ōamaru, open daily
  8.30am–4.30pm. Rainbow is the best manufacturer-direct route for small-business volumes. I found no formal trade-account
  page, so ask them about trade pricing.
- **Supermarket multi-buys:** New World often has Pascall 150–180g lines at 2 for $4 (about $2 each). PAK'nSAVE has had
  Party Pack at 2 for $5.
- **NXP:** free delivery on orders of $50 or more ex GST; orders under that pay a $6 fee.
- **Mondelēz (Pascall) direct to small businesses:** **unconfirmed**. I found nothing on this.

## What the owner must check

1. **MPI labelling for wrapped pieces.** Minties, Fruit Bursts, Milk Shakes and Mayceys Mints come out of a bulk bag.
   Ask MPI whether loose wrapped pieces meet the manufacturer-packaged exemption, given what the individual wrapper
   carries (name, allergens, best-before). If MPI says no, drop them and add a small sealed bag instead, such as a
   Rainbow Party Mix 110g at $1.50–$1.80.
2. **Bag fit.** No bag dimensions were published. Lay a Jet Planes 180g, Sour Feijoa 85g and Rainbow 110g bag flat in
   each box size.
3. **Live prices.** All prices are undated search snapshots.
4. **Best-before dates** need to be late February 2027 or later for December dispatch.
5. **Piece counts** for Fruit Bursts and Milk Shakes are estimates. Count one bag.
