# Sweet As Box: Facebook Page setup (about 15 minutes)

You need your own personal Facebook account to create a business Page. Your personal profile stays private.
Customers only see the Page. Facebook moves its menus around often, so if a button isn't exactly where described,
look for the same name nearby.

## What you need ready
| Item | File |
|---|---|
| Profile picture | `03 Social Media/sweet-as-box-instagram-profile.png` (red with the lolly) |
| Cover photo | `sweet-as-box-facebook-cover.png` (red), or `sweet-as-box-facebook-cover-cream.png` |
| First posts | `03 Social Media/Christmas launch posts/` plus `CAPTIONS.md` |

---

## Step 1: Create the Page
1. On a computer, log in to Facebook and go to **facebook.com/pages/create**.
2. **Page name:** `Sweet As Box`
3. **Category:** type `Gift Shop` and select it. Add `Candy Store` as a second category if it lets you.
4. **Bio**, copy and paste this (89 characters, under Facebook's 101 limit):
   > Kiwi classic treats, gift-boxed and posted NZ-wide. Secret Santa & staff gifts, sorted. 🎁
5. Click **Create Page**.

## Step 2: Add your pictures
1. **Profile picture:** upload `sweet-as-box-instagram-profile.png`. Facebook shows it as a circle, and the lolly is centred for that.
2. **Cover photo:** upload `sweet-as-box-facebook-cover.png`. Phones crop the sides off, so the logo and text are
   all in the middle where every screen shows them. See `cover-crop-check.png`.

## Step 3: Details
In the Page's **Edit details / Page setup** section:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Website | `https://sweetasbox.co.nz` (add it once your Shopify store is live) |
| Email | Your business email. A free Gmail like sweetasboxnz@gmail.com is fine |
| Phone | Optional. Leave it blank if you don't want calls |
| Location / Address | **Don't add your home address.** Choose "No physical location" or set service area to **New Zealand** |
| Hours | "Always open" (it's an online shop) |
| Price range | $$ |

## Step 4: Username (your Page web address)
Settings → **Username**. Try in this order: `sweetasbox` → `sweetasboxnz` → `sweetasbox.nz`.
Use the **same name as your Instagram** if possible, and update the @handle on your cards and posts to match.

## Step 5: Action button
Click **Add action button**:
- When the Shopify store is live: **Shop now**, linking to `https://sweetasbox.co.nz`
- Until then: **Send message**, so people can order or ask questions in Messenger

## Step 6: About section (longer description)
Paste this into **About / Description** if there's room for longer text:

> very own Kiwi Christmas Bingo card, then posting them anywhere in Aotearoa.
>
> 🎁 Mini box $35: Secret Santa sorted

>
> Summer-safe treats, packed by hand, with a handwritten message on every box.

## Step 7: Automatic Messenger replies
Open **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com) → **Inbox** → **Automations**.

**Instant reply** (sent when someone messages you for the first time):
> Kia ora, thanks for messaging Sweet As Box! 🎁 We'll get back to you within a few hours. Quick answers:
> Mini box $35 · Classic box $59 · posted NZ-wide · order for Christmas by 18 Dec (rural 15 Dec).

**Frequently asked questions** (buttons people can tap):
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How much are the boxes? | Mini box $35 (lollies, coffee, Kiwi Christmas Bingo). Classic box $59 (two lolly bags, Kiwi cookie, mānuka lip balm, coffee, bingo, handwritten message). Shipping $9.90 (rural $15.90). |
| When will it arrive? | We post Monday to Wednesday, usually 1–3 working days anywhere in NZ. Order by 18 Dec for Christmas (rural by 15 Dec). |
| Can I order for my team? | Yes! Send us your list of names and addresses and we'll post a box to each person with your company's message. From $35 per person, with bulk discounts for 10+ boxes. Order by 5 Dec. |

**Away message:** turn on for evenings so people know you'll reply in the morning.

## Step 8: Link Instagram
Meta Business Suite → **Settings** → **Instagram accounts / Linked accounts** → connect your Instagram.
After that you can post to both at once, schedule posts ahead, and see all messages in one inbox.

## Step 9: First posts
1. Schedule the 9 posts from `Christmas launch posts/` using the dates and captions in `CAPTIONS.md`.
   Meta Business Suite → **Planner** lets you schedule them all in one go.
2. Once the launch post is up, **pin it** to the top of your Page (⋯ on the post → Pin).

## Step 10: Get your first followers
1. **Invite your Facebook friends** to like the Page (Facebook prompts you after setup).
2. Share the launch post on your personal profile and ask a few friends to share it too.
3. Post in **NZ community and buy-and-sell groups** (local suburb groups, NZ small business groups).
   Read each group's rules first. Many allow business posts on certain days only.
4. Ask your makers to share the Page once they're confirmed.

## Keep it safe
- Turn on **two-factor authentication** on your personal Facebook account (Settings → Security). If your account
  gets hacked, you lose the Page too.
- Add a **second admin** you trust (Settings → Page access), so you're never locked out.
- Facebook will never message you saying your Page will be deleted unless you click a link. Those messages are scams.

## Later (optional)
- **Facebook & Instagram Shop:** connect Shopify's "Facebook & Instagram" sales channel so people can tap products in your posts.
- **Ads:** start the $10/day test from your plan once the Page has the 9 posts and some followers.
