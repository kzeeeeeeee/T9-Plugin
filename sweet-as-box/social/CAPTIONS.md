# Sweet As Box: Instagram posts, captions and schedule

9 feed posts at **1080×1350** (Instagram's 4:5 portrait size, which takes up the most room in the feed). Post them
on Instagram and Facebook at the same time. Each caption below is ready to copy and paste.

**Before you post:**
- Change **@sweetasbox** if your handle is different. It also appears on posts 1, 6 and 9.
- Check the prices ($35 Mini, $59 Classic), the box contents and the business-order deadline (5 Dec) still match
  your plan.
- Check the last-order dates on post 9 against **NZ Post's 2026 Christmas sending dates** once they're published.
- These are graphic posts. Once your boxes are packed, add **real photos and an unboxing video** between them.
  Real photos almost always get more engagement than graphics.

**Best time to post:** weekday evenings, 7–9pm NZ time.
