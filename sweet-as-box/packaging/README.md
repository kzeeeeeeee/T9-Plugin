# Sweet As Box: printed lid wrap

The Kiwi Christmas pattern covers the whole lid and wraps over the front edge, so the kraft box looks custom
printed. Each A4 sheet also gives you a matching gift tag and 2 round seal stickers.

## Files
| File | Box | What it is |
|---|---|---|

## What you need
- **A4 full-sheet sticker paper** (one whole sticky sheet, not cut into small labels), white, matte or gloss.
  Check the pack says it suits your printer (inkjet or laser).
- A colour printer, or a print shop (Warehouse Stationery, Snap, a local printer) that will print onto your sticker sheets.
- A paper trimmer or a sharp craft knife and steel ruler.

## Printing
1. Open the PDF and print at **Actual size / 100%**. Don't choose "Fit to page".
2. Print one test sheet first and hold the lid section against a box to check the size.

## Cutting
1. Cut along the **solid outer line** around the lid and red band.
2. Cut out the gift tag and the 2 round seals. They're optional extras from the same sheet.

## Applying it to the box
1. Fold the box and close the lid.
2. Peel the backing off the top edge only and line up the **dashed fold line** with the lid's front edge.
3. Smooth the sticker from the centre of the lid outwards, a little at a time, to avoid bubbles.
4. Fold the red band down over the front edge and press it flat.
5. Use a round seal over the front to hold the box closed, and tie on or stick the gift tag.
